Not long ago I felt it was time I started self-hosting services I use for a
variety of reasons.  I started on [Fly.io](https://fly.io) and launched a
[Ghost](https://ghost.org/) and I felt good about it.  That lasted about a month
at which point I realized that it was overkill and costing me money that was
better spent on other things.  That's when I stumbled across [this
post](https://www.mat.services/posts/static-site-with-nix-and-caddy/) by Mat
Ess.  Not only was the content excellent but the site was, to my eye,
beautiful.  I poked around and [read
more](https://www.mat.services/posts/now-on-netlify/) and eventually [found the
site's code](https://git.mat.services/mat/mat.services).  So, I've followed
Mat's example and adapted his work for my site.  Let me be clear, this saved
me a lot of time and effort.  I'll learn more as I go, but using his work I
was able to start quickly.  Thanks Mat.

Like Mat, I have other things running on Fly.io such as
[GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/) analytics instance, as well as a
[Forgejo](https://forgejo.org/) instance with [Drone](https://drone.io/).  What
Mat did to with [Nix](https://nixos.org/) flakes and Drone CI is well thought
out and works like a charm.

Nice job.  Thank you.  I appreciate that you were kind enough to publish your
work under a license that allowed me to benefit from your technical expertice
and design sense.
