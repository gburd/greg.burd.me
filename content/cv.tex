\section*{Employment}

\begin{itemize}

\item {\it Klar}, Director of Engineering -- Cambridge, MA -- July 2022 - current

Reduced database infrastructure costs by 60\% and aligned costs with customer demand. Reduced database incidents from weekly to infrequent through education and hands-on changes to the ~100 microservices.  Introduced a new functions-style method for delivering on data engineering tasks reducing complexity for engineers better focused on analysis.

\item {\it BlockFi}, Senior Staff Software Engineer -- Cambridge, MA -- June 2020 - June 2022

Designed and delivered observability stack at BlockFi (logs, metrics, tracing, alerts), grew the team to 8 then handed it off to a team member I groomed and promoted. I joined the data storage team and oversaw the health of Postgres as well as other data engineering issues.

\item {\it Humio}, Principal Software Engineer -- Cambridge, MA -- September 2018 - April 2020

I worked on the backend team to create an innovative index-free filter-based storage system able to find needles spread across multi-petabyte archives of log and metric data by intelligently filtering data to reduce IO to the absolute minimum and then exploit modern CPU/memory architecture despite being on the JVM (Scala).

\item {\it Onshape}, Principal Software Engineer, Architect -- Cambridge, MA -- January 2017 - October 2018

I worked on data storage responsible for primary, object, and long-term storage as well as caching.  I was responsible for taking Onshape from the pre-launch/1.0 data architecture based entirely on MongoDB and MemcacheD to something that will provide more consistent performance at scale.  To that end, I authored a new client layer that used Cassandra while providing causal consistency and in-application caching and nesting transactions for accessing model objects via a friendly Java-based DSL (https://github.com/gburd/helenus and https://github.com/gburd/memcached-jcache).

\item {\it Akamai Technologies}, Senior Software Development Engineer -- Cambridge, MA -- July 2015 - December 2016

I was a member of the Labs group at Akamai. I owned the database component (design, implementation, deployment, automation, and ongoing management) for a WebRTC-based peer and multi-party video conferencing platform. In addition, I customized a Linux distro to provide an always-on, low-maintenance kiosk-style video-wall experience combining an Intel NUC and large television and deployed this into Akamai's lobby as well as other locations within the company.

\item {\it Amazon AWS}, Senior Software Development Engineer -- Cambridge, MA -- July 2014 - July 2015

I wrote and deployed software for the Storage Gateway service’s data and control plane as well as for the virtualized on-premise iSCSI gateway. I orchestrated the zero-downtime upgrade of thousands of customers remotely to mitigate the Bash ShellShock bug. I investigated and produced a design to incorporate de-duplication techniques to lower the overhead of storage and speed up the time to perform backups of customer data into the service’s data plane. I represented our service in weekly operations meetings and performed root cause analysis for many system failures.

\item {\it Basho Technologies}, Software Architect and Product Manager -- Cambridge, MA -- Jul 2011 - Apr 2014

I authored node-level data storage and asynchronous thread queues for Riak in Erlang and C. I identified TCP node-level communication using \si{\micro}TP. I built a layer to isolate Erlang’s BEAM virtual machine from native code to avoid “scheduler collapse”. I investigated alternatives to TCP for intra-node work, specifically reviewing  \si{\micro}TP (micro transport protocol, used by BitTorrent). I built a pure-Erlang local LSM-style ordered storage layer called HanoiDB and investigated other local storage alternatives.
