## greg(ory) burd

I'm a software engineer. I have been for 35+ years.

These days I write C and Rust full-time, mostly on PostgreSQL — both
contributing to the upstream project and building extensions
([pg_mentat](https://github.com/gburd/pg_mentat),
[pg_infer](https://codeberg.org/gregburd/pg_infer),
[pg_tre](https://codeberg.org/gregburd/pg_tre), and others).
By day I'm a senior engineer at Amazon Web Services on the Amazon
DocumentDB team. The opinions on this site are mine, not theirs.

You can read the long version on [my resume](/resume), or skim a
[selected list of projects](/projects).

I don't have "spare time" — I have four kids who are amazing,
demanding, and way too much fun to ignore.
